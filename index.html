The Perfect 2-Minute Maggi

Ingredients

For the noodles:

  • 1 packet of instant noodles
  • 1½ cups water
  • 1 tastemaker sachet

For the extras:

  • 1 egg
  • A handful of chopped veggies
  • A little butter

Instructions

  1. Boil the water in a pan.
  2. Break the noodles in and add the tastemaker.
  3. Add the veggies and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Crack in the egg and stir.
  5. Add butter, serve hot and enjoy.
